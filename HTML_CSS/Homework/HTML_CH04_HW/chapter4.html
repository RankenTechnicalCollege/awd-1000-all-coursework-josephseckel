Why I Like Cars

Cars are interesting to me because my grandfather had over 100 and I grew up around them. I also enjoy learning about the history of cars and how they have changed over the years.

I especially like learning about rare imported cars and how they are different from American cars. I also enjoy learning about the different types of engines and how they work.
